AI AURA & GENZURA AVATARS - SETUP
=================================

KYA HAI?
- Profile par naya button "✨ My Aura". Us se aap ka Aura + 3D-style Avatar banta hai.
- AURA = 2 colors + 2-4 traits (jaise "🎧 Music Soul", "🌙 Night Owl", "🔥 Consistent Creator", "⚡ Reel Addict", "😎 Emoji Fan").
  Ye aap ke apne uploads/posts se phone par hi nikalta hai: sab se zyada category (Music samet), post karne ka waqt,
  kitne din active, reels ka hissa, captions mein emojis. "Aura name" jaise "Midnight Violet Aura".
- AVATAR = original SVG artwork (copyright ka koi masla nahi): 6 skin tone, 8 hairstyle, 6 hair color, 8 accessories
  (glasses, headphones, cap, shades, crown, headband, earrings). Drag karo to 3D mein ghoomta hai. Shuffle bhi hai.
- 30 READY-MADE AVATARS aura ke hisaab se (aura sheet mein "30 Avatars" gallery):
    🎧 Music (4): DJ Violet, Lo-fi Chill, Rockstar, Singer Rose
    🎮 Gaming (4): Pro Gamer, Stream King, Pixel Girl, Night Raider
    😂 Comedy (3): Meme Lord, Jester, Laugh Riot
    🏏 Sports (3): Cricket Captain, Gym Hero, Striker
    🧠 Curious (3): Bookworm, Coder, Scientist
    🌍 Travel (3): Backpacker, Beach Wanderer, Desert Explorer
    🎬 Film (3): Director, Cinephile, Animator
    🌟 Star (3): Star, Glam Queen, Showman
    📰 News (2): Anchor, Reporter
    💬 Vibe (2): Sunny Soul, Chill Buddy
  "⭐ For me" tab mein pehle wo avatars aate hain jo aap ki aura traits se match karte hain. Naye user ka pehla avatar bhi
  usi theme ke 30 mein se khud chun liya jata hai. Har avatar aap ke apne aura colors mein dikhta hai. Tap karo = lag gaya, phir Save.
- DOSTON KI FEED PAR REACT: Home ke upar (stories ke neeche) friends ke avatars ki strip aati hai.
  Dost ka avatar un ke Mood / Vibe ke hisab se expression badalta hai aur ek emoji bubble dikhata hai:
    Sleeping 😴 -> neend wala chehra     Hungry 🍔 -> zubaan bahar      Gaming 🎮 / Cramming 📚 / Chai ☕ / Movie 🍿 / Walk 🌙 ...
  Mood na ho to normal khush chehra. Tap karo to un ka aura card khulta hai.
- "Show my avatar to friends" switch se koi apna avatar chhupa sakta hai.
- Friends = mutual follow ya phone-contacts (jaise Vibe feature mein).

IMPORTANT (sach)
- Ye rule-based "AI" hai: koi external AI model / API use nahi hota, isliye kharcha zero hai aur data phone se bahar nahi jata.
  Asli generative AI (photo se avatar, AI se likha aura) ke liye Netlify function + AI API key chahiye hogi - alag step.
- "Music taste" ke liye abhi app mein music-listening data nahi hai, isliye ye aap ke "Music" category ke videos se nikalta hai.

STEP 1 - FIREBASE RULES [2 minute]
- Is zip ki firestore.rules mein "auras" ka naya block hai (Near Me block ke baad, "EVERYTHING ELSE IS DENIED" se pehle).
- Firebase Console -> Firestore Database -> Rules -> poori purani rules mita ke nayi firestore.rules paste -> Publish.
- Koi index nahi chahiye.

STEP 2 - DEPLOY
- Web zip Netlify par drop karo (service-worker cache v78). Android app mein koi change nahi chahiye
  (Android zip sirf Near Me ke liye pehle wali hi use hoti hai).
