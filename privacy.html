Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gupzy ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and mobile application (together, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign up or log in (via Google Sign-In or email/password), we collect your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Content you provide: Videos, reels, comments, likes, subscriptions, channel details, and any other content you upload or create using the Service.
  • Camera and microphone: Used only while you use a feature that needs them: voice and video calls, recording videos and stories, voice messages, Real Moments, Side Quest clips, Soundbite Maker and group voice rooms. If live streaming (Go Live) is released, it will use them only while you broadcast.
  • Usage data: Basic interaction data such as videos viewed, likes, shares, watch time, and device/browser type, used to operate and improve the Service.
  • Messages and chat data: Messages, photos, voice messages and videos you send in chats and groups. They are end-to-end encrypted (see “Chats and groups” below). We also keep chat metadata such as who is in a chat, when the last message was sent and by whom.
  • Status and activity information: Mood, do-not-disturb hours, vibe, online status and similar settings you turn on.
  • Approximate location (optional): Only if you use Near Me or tick “share my approximate location” in the group Ledger.
  • Technical information: Your IP address, which our network provider (Cloudflare) uses at the moment of a request to work out your country and to detect VPN/proxy use; your device time zone; and, in the Android app, a yes/no signal whether a VPN is switched on.
  • Payout and identity information (only if you withdraw earnings): see “Monetization, Gupzy Studio and payouts” below.

Contacts (Android app, optional)

If you allow contacts access (the app asks when you first open it, or when you use "Find friends from your contacts"), the app reads the phone numbers and e-mail addresses in your address book on your device and converts each one into a one-way hash before sending it to our server; this sync then repeats automatically in the background of normal app use. We never upload contact names or raw numbers. The names saved in your address book are read on your device only, to label your own chats and calls and notifications; they are never sent to our server and never shown on anyone's profile. We use the hashes only to find people who are on Gupzy and have also saved your number, so you can chat and call without following each other. Your verified Google e-mail (and phone number, if you added one) is stored only as a hash. You can stop at any time from the Contacts screen, which deletes your contact data and links.

To work out who is a mutual contact, our server also keeps (as hashes only) a list of the contacts you synced and a hashed identity for your account. This is how we match you with people who have saved your number, how the “Who saved me” count works, and how contact links are created between people who have each other. This list is not readable by other users.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features: video/reel playback, uploads, comments, likes, subscriptions, chats, calls, notifications and the friend features described below (and live streaming once it is released).
  • To maintain your account and authenticate you securely.
  • To improve app performance, fix issues, and personalize the content feed.
  • To display advertising, as described below.
  • To count views by country, detect VPN/proxy and fake activity, and calculate creator earnings fairly.
  • To keep the Service safe: review content, handle reports and appeals, prevent abuse, fraud and spam, and meet legal obligations.
  • To help you: answer support emails, send notifications you have not turned off, and provide translation and Gupzy AI when you ask for them.

3. Advertising & Cookies

Ads are shown inside the Gupzy Android app through Google AdMob (including the optional rewarded ad you can watch to boost a reel). The Gupzy website does not currently show third-party ads. If we add web ads (for example Google AdSense or other ad networks), those providers may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites/apps, and we will update this policy.

You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad preferences at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Data Storage & Security

Account and content data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Storage services). Photos, videos and audio are stored with Cloudinary, and our website and server functions run on Cloudflare. Chat messages and chat media are end-to-end encrypted before they leave your device. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the app (listed in “Service providers we use” below).
  • Other users, as described feature by feature below (for example your public channel and content, and what you choose to show your friends).
  • Advertising partners (Google AdMob/AdSense), which may receive device identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in section 3.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

6. Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete your account content from within the app.
  • You can revoke camera/microphone permissions at any time from your device settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising via your Google account ad settings.
  • You can turn off or change: your mood, do-not-disturb and vibe status; showing your online status; showing your avatar to friends; Ghost Meter (also stops the counting); Crush Vote; and contacts sync (Contacts screen, which deletes your contact data and links).
  • You can deny or revoke location, contacts, camera, microphone and notification permissions in your device settings, and clear your Gupzy AI chat with the bin icon.
  • You can delete your own posts, stories, Real Moments and Near Me posts yourself, and block or report other users.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Additional information we process

Location (Android app, optional): If you use the "Near Me" feature and allow location access, we use your approximate location to show and publish local posts and events (posts show a roughly 1 km area and expire after 24 hours). You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

Screen sharing: Screen sharing during calls starts only after you tap start and accept the Android system prompt. It is sent directly to the person you are in a call with.

Calls and chats: Voice/video calls use WebRTC and relay (TURN) servers to connect devices. Call media is encrypted in transit and is not recorded by us, but calls are not end-to-end encrypted by Gupzy. Chat messages are protected with end-to-end encryption keys; we store your public key and an encrypted key backup.

AI features: Features such as Aura are worked out on your phone from your activity in the app.

Translation: If you tap translate, the text of that caption or comment is sent to a translation provider to return the translation.

Media uploads: Photos and videos you upload are stored with our media provider (Cloudinary).

Monetization and payouts: If you request a withdrawal, we store the identity and payout details you provide (see “Monetization, Gupzy Studio and payouts” below).

Push notifications: We store a notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) for your device.

How each feature uses your information

“Friends” below means people you follow who also follow you, or people linked to you through contacts sync. Features marked “optional” only collect data after you turn them on.

Account and channel. Your channel name, handle, logo and the content you publish are visible to other users. You can edit your channel or delete your account at any time.

Videos, reels, posts and stories. New videos and posts are reviewed by our team before they are shown (see “Content review and moderation”). Comments, likes, follows and tags you make can be seen by the other person and, for public items, by other users. Saved items are private to you. Stories disappear after 24 hours; the story owner can see who viewed the story. If you save stories to highlights or the story archive they stay until you remove them. Photos, videos and audio are stored with Cloudinary.

Views, watch activity and your feed. When you watch a video or reel we record a view: your account ID, the video, whether it came from the app or the web, the country (worked out from your IP address by Cloudflare, not stored as an address) and whether VPN/proxy was detected and why (for example a hosting or VPN network, Tor, a time zone that does not match, or a VPN switched on in the Android app). If we use an optional IP-reputation service (such as proxycheck.io or vpnapi.io), your IP address is sent to it for that check. Views, shares and watch time are also used to personalise your feed and to count how many reels you watched for at least 3 seconds (used for monetization requirements). “No-Algorithm mode” shows only people you follow, newest first, and hides promoted reels.

Boost and paid promotion. Boost lets you watch a short rewarded ad (Google AdMob) before uploading so your reel is placed at the top of Reels for 5 minutes. Paid promotion is arranged by email only (see section 9).

Chats and groups. Chats and groups (up to 50 members) are end-to-end encrypted: each device has its own key pair, the private key stays on your device, and our servers store only encrypted messages. Photos, voice messages and videos are encrypted on your phone before upload, so our media provider only holds unreadable files. We can see metadata such as who is in a chat, timestamps, group names and photos, and the last sender/time. Push notifications for chats use generic text. Chat backup is protected by a backup password you choose; we cannot recover it, and if you forget it old messages cannot be restored by anyone, including us. If a friend reinstalls without a backup, old messages with them can become unreadable. Disappearing messages (1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days) are a best-effort setting and cannot stop someone from copying or taking a screenshot before a message disappears.

Gupchats. Gupchats is a companion chat app that uses the same account, chats, groups, stories, calls and keys as Gupzy. It also stores an online-status record while the app is open (you can turn “Show when I’m online” off, which also hides others from you) and a notification token for the Gupchats app.

Voice and video calls. Calls are possible only between people who follow each other or are mutual contacts. We store the call request and connection set-up data needed to ring and connect the phones. The audio/video goes directly between devices or through a relay (TURN) server and is not recorded. The other person and relay servers can see your network address as part of making the connection. Screen sharing starts only after you tap start. If you have do-not-disturb on, calls from people who are not Close Friends are declined silently and you see a count later.

SMS when you have no internet (Android, optional). You can open your SMS app with a pre-filled message to a number you type. That number is saved encrypted on your phone only and is never uploaded. The SMS goes through your mobile network, is not end-to-end encrypted and uses your SIM balance.

Mood, do-not-disturb, vibe and online status (optional). We store the mood or vibe you choose (for example Busy, Sleeping, Down for Chai), its expiry (vibes last at most 2 hours), your quiet hours and your time zone. Friends see your mood or vibe on your profile, in chat and in a floating strip at the top of Messages. When you and two friends share the same vibe, a temporary group chat can be created automatically unless you opt out. Do-not-disturb is enforced on your phone and on our server so notifications and calls stay silent, except for people on your Close Friends list.

Close Friends. The list is private to you and is used for Stories and for do-not-disturb exceptions.

Real Moments (optional). Once a day at a random time (between 10:00 and 21:00 Pakistan time) everyone gets a notification and has 2 minutes to post. We store the live photos from your back and front cameras (no gallery or filters), your short caption, whether you were late, and the time. Friends can see your moment after they post their own. You can delete a moment yourself. No location is collected.

Near Me (optional). When you open Near Me and allow location, the app uses your location to find posts and events within 5 or 10 km. A post stores only a rough area of about 1 km, plus your text, optional photo and name, and expires after 24 hours. For an event you choose a precise venue on the map and that venue is public, so do not enter your home address. Anyone nearby can see your post or event, and you can delete it. Map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, which can see your IP address and the area you view.

My Aura and avatars (optional). Your aura (colours and traits such as “Night Owl”) is worked out on your phone from your own uploads and posts (main category, posting time, active days, share of reels, emoji use). We store only the colours, traits and avatar choices you save. Friends can see your avatar, which can change expression with your mood or vibe; you can hide it. This does not use an external AI service.

Ghost Meter (optional). When you reply in a 1-to-1 chat, your phone measures only the time between their last message and your reply (never the text) and keeps your last 20 reply times. With enough replies a badge such as Super Responder or Certified Ghost can show next to your name. You can turn it off, which also stops counting.

Crush Vote, Sparks and Who saved me (optional). If you turn Crush Vote on, friends can anonymously vote Secret Crush or Legend; you only see totals. Each vote can be read only by the person who cast it. If two people vote Secret Crush for each other, a small “Spark” shows on both profiles, without a notification. “Who saved me” shows you one number (hidden if fewer than 3) of people who saved your number, never names; it uses the contact hashes described above.

Side Quest. Each day you get a local quest. If you choose to take part, you record a clip of up to 5 seconds, confirm that everyone filmed agreed, and earn points and streak days. The clip, your points and your streak are stored; clips are currently shown only in your own quest log. Your location is never collected.

Co-Reel (Watch Party) and Truth-Dare-Roast Spin. These create a room with a secret code that stores the host and guests (IDs and names), the current reel and the room state. Up to 4 people watch together; up to 6 join a voice call for the wheel game. Voice goes device to device or through a relay and is not recorded. Rooms older than a few hours can no longer be joined.

Shared Albums. Album members can add photos and short videos. They are stored with Cloudinary and visible to the members of that album.

Soundbite Maker. Voice clips you record or pick are uploaded, mixed with a beat or effect, and given a share link. Anyone with the link can play it.

Daku Ledger (group chats). Group members can post “I’m coming in N min” notes, press Busted, log Udhaar (things like chai or money owed) and see a leaderboard. Entries are visible to that group. Location is optional: it is attached, rounded to about 100 m, only if you tick “share my approx. location”.

Creator Tip Jar (optional). If you turn it on, the EasyPaisa or JazzCash number, account title and thank-you message you enter are shown on your public profile with a Tip button. Tips go directly from person to person; Gupzy does not process or see those payments.

Referrals. When someone joins through your referral link we store a referral record (your ID, the new channel, the date). It is used to check monetization requirements.

Notifications. We store in-app notification records and a notification token for each device (Gupzy, Gupchats) so we can send push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

Translation. When you tap Translate, the text of that caption or comment is sent through our server to Google’s translation service (or, if that fails, MyMemory) and the result is returned to you.

Reports, appeals and moderation records. Reports, appeals and moderation decisions are stored so we can act on them, handle repeat violations and meet legal requests.

Monetization, Gupzy Studio and payouts

Gupzy Studio (studio.gupzy.com and its Android app) is the creator dashboard that uses your Gupzy account. It shows your analytics, views by country, estimated earnings, referrals and withdrawals. Creator earnings come from ad revenue shared by views, country by country (you receive 70% of your share). A channel becomes monetized automatically when the requirements in Studio are met.

When you request your first withdrawal we ask for and store: your phone number, address, gender and country (if on your channel), your national ID (CNIC) number and a photo of its front side, and your payout details (bank, JazzCash or EasyPaisa name, account title and number, or Payoneer). We also store each withdrawal request, its amount and status. We use this to verify identity, prevent fraud, pay you, keep accounting records and meet legal obligations. Only authorised team members can see it, and it is never copied to your public profile or Tip Jar. We keep it for as long as needed for payments, fraud prevention, accounting and the law.

Service providers we use

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: sign-in (Google Sign-In, email/password), database, notifications (Cloud Messaging).
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, network security, and the servers that send push notifications, count views by country, sync contacts, translate text and run Gupzy AI.
  • Cloudinary: storage and delivery of photos, videos and audio (chat media is encrypted before upload).
  • Google AdMob: ads and rewarded ads inside the Android app.
  • Groq: the AI provider that answers Gupzy AI messages.
  • Google Translate and MyMemory: translation.
  • ExpressTURN and Google STUN servers: help connect voice and video calls.
  • OpenStreetMap: map pictures in Near Me.
  • proxycheck.io / vpnapi.io (optional): IP-reputation check used only to detect VPN or proxy views.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us and have their own policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Android app permissions

The Android app may ask for: camera and microphone (calls, recording, Real Moments, Side Quest, voice messages); contacts (optional, for finding friends); location (optional, only for Near Me and the optional Ledger location); notifications; and network state (to check whether a VPN is on). You can say no or change these at any time in your device settings; the related feature then will not work.

Legal bases (EU, UK and similar laws)

We process your data to provide the Service you asked for (contract), for our legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and improving the Service, with your consent where required (for example contacts, location, camera and microphone permissions, and personalised ads), and to meet legal obligations.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account inside the app: Profile menu → Delete my account. You can also request deletion from outside the app at gupzy.com/delete-account.html.

Removed straight away by the in-app button: your channel and profile, videos, posts, stories, Near Me posts, follows, saved items, notifications sent to you, notification tokens, chat encryption keys and key backups, online, mood and vibe status, Close Friends list, Crush Vote setting, aura/avatar settings, Tip Jar details, and your sign-in.

Not removed automatically by the button (email us and we will delete it within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it): comments and likes you made, view records, Real Moments, Side Quest history and points, Ghost Meter statistics, Crush votes and Sparks, contacts-sync data (you can also remove it yourself with Stop sync on the Contacts screen before deleting), your items in shared albums, Soundbite links, Ledger entries, room records, referral records, and chat messages. Chat messages are end-to-end encrypted; once your keys are deleted you can no longer read them, but other members may still have their own copies of conversations.

Monetization applications and withdrawal records (including payout and ID details) are kept for as long as needed for payments, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations. Some data may also remain for a short time in backups or where the law requires us to keep it (for example, reports of abuse and moderation records).

Reporting, blocking and safety

You can report videos, posts, stories, local posts and users inside the app, and block other users. We review reports and remove content that breaks our Terms or the law.

Gupzy AI

Gupzy AI is an AI assistant inside the chat list. What you type to Gupzy AI is sent through our server to an AI provider (Groq) so it can answer. These messages are not end-to-end encrypted and are not shared with other users. The chat history is kept only on your own device, and you can clear it with the bin icon. With each question, the last part of your conversation (up to 12 messages) is sent so the AI can answer in context, and the number of messages you can send in a short time is limited. Gupzy AI can be wrong and does not give professional advice. Please do not send passwords or other secrets to Gupzy AI.

Content review and moderation

New videos and posts are reviewed by our team before they are shown to other people. Our reviewers can see the content you upload and the reports sent about it. If content is removed, we keep a record (what it was, who posted it, when it was uploaded and removed, and why) so we can handle appeals, repeat violations and legal requests.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies, browser storage and a service worker on the website for sign-in, to remember your settings and to make the app load faster. Ad providers such as Google may also use cookies or device identifiers to show and measure ads (see section 3). You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time, but sign-in may stop working. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland) we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for certain purposes, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority. You can delete your account yourself in the app, or email gupzysupport@gmail.com for any other request. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context advertising other than the Google ad services described above.

Where your data is stored and how long we keep it

Your data is processed by our service providers (Google Firebase / Google Cloud, Cloudinary and Cloudflare), whose servers can be in countries other than yours, including the United States. We keep your data while your account is active and delete it when you delete your account, except for limited records we must keep for safety or by law (for example abuse reports and moderation records) and short-term backups.

Security incidents

If a data breach affects your personal information, we will take steps to contain it and will tell the affected people and the authorities where the law requires.
