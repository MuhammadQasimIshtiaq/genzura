GUPCHATS v4 - VOICE + VIDEO CALLS (same call system as Gupzy)

What changed
- Gupchats chat screen now has a voice-call and a video-call button (top right).
- Calls use the SAME Firestore "calls" documents, TURN servers and push as Gupzy, so:
    Gupchats -> Gupzy, Gupzy -> Gupchats and Gupchats -> Gupchats all work. The other person does NOT need Gupchats.
- Incoming call: full-screen ringing notification (Answer / Decline) with the phone locked or the app closed,
  in-app incoming screen when the app is open. Ongoing call keeps running in the background (foreground service).
- Mute, speaker/earpiece, camera on/off, flip camera. Back button never kills a call (Android: minimises it).
- Same rule as Gupzy: you can only call people you follow / who follow you (mutual) or mutual phone contacts.

NOTES
- If BOTH Gupzy and Gupchats are installed and signed in on one phone, a closed-app call rings in both apps.
  Answer in one; the other notification stops by itself after at most 45 s.
- Gupzy Android app: NO change needed. Gupzy website: NO change needed.
- Calls are not end-to-end encrypted by this feature (WebRTC media is encrypted in transit, as in Gupzy).
