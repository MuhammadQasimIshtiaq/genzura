About Us

Last updated: October 2026

Gupzy is a video-sharing and social platform where people can watch videos, share reels and stories, post photos, chat with friends, and go live — all in one place, on the web and on Android.

What you can do on Gupzy

  • Watch and upload videos, reels and stories.
  • Create a channel, subscribe to creators, like and comment.
  • Send messages, voice messages, and make audio or video calls.
  • Go live and connect with your audience.

Our goal

We want to give creators, especially new and small ones, an easy and friendly place to share their talent and be discovered, with a safe and respectful community.

Community & safety

We expect everyone to follow our Terms & Conditions and respect the rights of others. You can report content or accounts from inside the app, or contact us using the details below.
