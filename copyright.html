Copyright & Reporting

Last updated: October 2026

Genzura respects the rights of creators and expects its users to do the same. Only upload content that you created or that you have permission to use.

Report a video, reel, account or comment

Use the report option inside the app on the content you want to flag. You can also email us the link to the content and the reason for your report.

Copyright complaints

If you believe your copyrighted work has been posted on Genzura without your permission, email genzurasupport@gmail.com with:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • A description of the original work that you say was copied.
  • The link(s) to the content on Genzura that you want reviewed.
  • A statement that you are the owner of the work or are authorised to act for the owner.

We review reports and may remove content that infringes copyright or breaks our Terms & Conditions.

Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly post infringing content may be suspended or closed.
