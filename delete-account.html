Delete your Gupzy account

Inside the app (fastest)

Open Gupzy → Profile → menu → Delete my account → confirm. Your account and content are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email gupzysupport@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Gupzy account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your channel and profile, videos, posts, stories, follows, saved items, notification tokens and chat encryption keys. Contact-sync data is deleted when you stop contacts sync or delete your account. Limited records may be kept where required by law (for example, abuse reports).
