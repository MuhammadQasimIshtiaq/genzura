Delete your Gupzy account

Inside the app (fastest)

Open Gupzy → Profile → menu → Delete my account → confirm. Your account and content are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email gupzysupport@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Gupzy account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted straight away (in-app button)

Your channel and profile, videos, posts, stories, Near Me posts, follows, saved items, notifications sent to you, notification tokens, chat encryption keys and key backups, online, mood and vibe status, Close Friends list, Crush Vote setting, aura/avatar settings, Tip Jar details, and your sign-in.

What we delete when you email us

Comments and likes you made, view records, Real Moments, Side Quest history and points, Ghost Meter statistics, Crush votes and Sparks, contacts-sync data (you can also use Stop sync on the Contacts screen), items in shared albums, Soundbite links, Ledger entries, room records, referral records and chat messages. Chat messages are end-to-end encrypted: after your keys are deleted you can no longer read them, but other people may still have their own copies of the conversation. Email gupzysupport@gmail.com and we will delete this data within 30 days.

What we may keep

Monetization applications and withdrawal records (including payout and ID details) are kept for as long as needed for payments, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations. Limited records may be kept where required by law (for example, abuse reports and moderation records), and data can stay for a short time in backups.
